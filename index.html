ABOUT

Research & Professional Profile

I am a Senior Researcher at the PQC Centre of Excellence, QNu Labs, Bengaluru, working on research and engineering challenges in post-quantum cryptography and hardware security.

My research interests include secure implementation of post-quantum cryptographic algorithms, side-channel attacks, physical leakage assessment, cryptographic hardware, and implementation-level countermeasures.

My current work includes the study and evaluation of lattice-based cryptographic primitives, particularly ML-KEM (Kyber) and ML-DSA (Dilithium), with a focus on practical security evaluation and implementation.
